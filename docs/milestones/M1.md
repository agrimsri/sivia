- **Date:** 2026-09-28

## 1. Goal (Restate)
Turn raw webcam/phone videos into a clean, diverse, deduplicated image pool with quality metadata, session-level partitioning, and a searchable DINOv2 embedding index.

---

## 2. What Was Built
1. **Interactive Recording Helper ([`record_helper.py`](../../scripts/record_helper.py)):** OpenCV webcam tool with live on-screen checklist for the 6 kit objects, recording controls (`[SPACE]`, `[Q]`, `[N]`), and automated session metadata emission (`conditions`, `resolution`, `fps`).
2. **Video Ingest ([`video_ingest.py`](../../src/sivia/capture/video_ingest.py)):** Decodes container metadata and yields frames with sequential frame index and precise second-level timestamps.
3. **Dual Frame Sampler ([`frame_sampler.py`](../../src/sivia/capture/frame_sampler.py)):** Samples at fixed 2 FPS combined with scene-change triggering based on grayscale frame differences.
4. **Quality Filter Engine ([`quality_filter.py`](../../src/sivia/capture/quality_filter.py)):** Computes Laplacian variance (sharpness) and mean luminance to reject blurred or underexposed/overexposed frames.
5. **DINOv2 Embedder ([`dino.py`](../../src/sivia/embeddings/dino.py)):** Extracts 384-dimensional L2-normalized CLS token embeddings from DINOv2 ViT-S/14.
6. **Two-Stage Deduplication ([`dedup.py`](../../src/sivia/capture/dedup.py)):**
   - Stage 1: Perceptual hash (pHash) exact and near-exact duplicate detection.
   - Stage 2: FAISS cosine radius clustering for semantic redundancy, always retaining the sharpest exemplar per cluster.
7. **Vector Index ([`faiss_index.py`](../../src/sivia/embeddings/faiss_index.py)):** Persistent IndexIDMap2 cosine index mapped directly to SQLite database sample IDs.
8. **Session Splitter ([`session_split.py`](../../src/sivia/capture/session_split.py)):** Strict session-level partitioning (70% Train, 15% Val, 15% Test) guaranteeing zero frame leakage, and exporting `data/splits/frozen_test.txt`.
9. **EDA Generator ([`generate_dataset_eda.py`](../../scripts/generate_dataset_eda.py) & [`m1_dataset_eda.ipynb`](../../notebooks/m1_dataset_eda.ipynb)):** Generates brightness/blur histograms and 2D PCA embedding cluster plots colored by session.
10. **Synthetic Video Benchmark Generator ([`generate_synthetic_videos.py`](../../scripts/generate_synthetic_videos.py)):** Automates generation of 20 distinct session videos across varied lighting and moving kit objects.
